— FAQ

Before you sign.

How much does it cost?

An audit (Mapping) costs CHF 5,000 to 9,000 and is delivered in four weeks. A full design and deployment engagement costs CHF 40,000 to 120,000, depending on the size of the organisation and the scope. No subscription, no licence.

How long does it take?

Three to six months for a full engagement. At every stage — mapping, architecture, build, handover — you decide whether to continue or stop.

What if I want to leave?

You own the system from day one: code, database, server and documentation. Any competent developer can take it over.

What size of company do you work with?

Swiss SMEs of 5 to 80 people. Below that, a full back office rarely pays off: we suggest a light audit instead. Above that, we work alongside your IT department.

What happens after delivery?

The system runs on its own and your team knows how to evolve it. A new module, a yearly audit or training remain possible, billed by the hour, never as a subscription.
